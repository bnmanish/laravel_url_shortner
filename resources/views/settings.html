How to add a custom domain
  1. Purchase a domain from any registrar (e.g., GoDaddy, Namecheap).
  2. Add the domain here and we'll give you a CNAME record.
  3. Add the CNAME record to your DNS provider pointing to cname.linkly.to.
  4. Wait for DNS propagation (usually 5-30 minutes) and your domain will be active.
